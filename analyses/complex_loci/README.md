# Complex Loci & Centromeric Regions

Targeted resolution and comparative analysis of previously inaccessible genomic features, including centromeric satellite arrays, major histocompatibility complex (MHC) immune loci, and sex chromosomes across diverse primate clades.

**Inputs:** T2T-complete haplotype assemblies (`genome_hap1`/`genome_hap2`) and repeat/annotation tracks (`annotation_gff3`) from `../../manifests/sample_data_manifest.csv`.

**Outputs:** centromere/satellite annotation tracks, MHC locus comparisons, sex chromosome structural summaries.
